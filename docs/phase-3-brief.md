# Phase 3 brief: services and trust (no Senate content)

For approval. Work happens on branch `phase-3-services-trust`; nothing reaches `main`
(and so Lovable) until you approve the result. Effort: M to L, mostly depending on
how quickly the inputs below arrive.

Goal from the work plan: show depth and credibility using only material Apexon can
stand behind today.

## What will be built

**1. Four service pages** (`/services/networking`, `/services/infrastructure`,
`/services/iptv-av`, `/services/maintenance`)

Each page covers: the problem it solves, what we supply and install, our process,
the standards and tests we work to, what the client receives at handover, and a
short FAQ. Each ends with "Request a quote" (service pre-selected) and WhatsApp.
The existing `/services` page becomes an overview linking to them.

**2. Handover pack page** (`/handover`) with downloadable samples, clearly marked
"SAMPLE: made-up data":

- cable test report
- labelling scheme
- as-built drawing
- handover checklist

**3. Compliance and documents page** (`/compliance`)

- Lists the company documents you hold, each marked "available on request".
- A short request form (uses the existing enquiry system with a new "documents" type).
- No badge or certification you do not hold. Warranty and maintenance terms appear
  only once you confirm them.

**4. Who we serve** sections for government, corporate and technology partners, plus
a **partners and subcontractors** enquiry form (new "partner" type).

**5. FAQ page** (`/faq`) gathering the most common questions, built with accessible
expand/collapse sections.

**6. Capability statement** for tender packs: a downloadable PDF generated from the
same content as the site, so it never contradicts it.

**7. Service area statement**: Nairobi base, travel elsewhere in Kenya (wording to
confirm), plus the confirmed address.

## What will not change

- No projects page, client names, testimonials or real project photos (parked).
- No Senate content anywhere.
- No new third-party scripts. Design and Phase 1/2 features stay as they are.

## Done when

- You have approved every page's wording.
- Every claim is on the claims checklist (work plan section 3) and you can prove it.
- Samples and the capability statement open correctly on phone and desktop.
- The new forms pass the same tests as Phase 2; accessibility and keyboard tests pass.
