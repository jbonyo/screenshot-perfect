How labels are built

Every cable carries the same label at both ends, and the outlet and patch-panel port show it too:

PartMeaningExample
F2Floor numberF2 = second floor
R01Rack or cabinet on that floorR01 = first rack
P1Patch panel in that rack, counted from the topP1 = top panel
01Port number on the panel01 = first port

Full label: F2-R01-P1-01 = floor 2, rack 1, panel 1, port 1.
